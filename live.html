Live Scores

Nothing is being scored live right now. Whoever runs the next Stableford or Strokeplay round can switch this on - see the README's “Live weekly singles scores” section. In the meantime, the finished 2026 standings are on the season hub and the Strokeplay Singles page, and team match play already updates itself from the captains' form on the match play page.
How this works. Whoever runs the week's round submits each player's gross score (and, for Stableford, their points) from a form after the round. An automated job reads the form the moment it is submitted and republishes this page - nobody needs a PC, and nobody needs to remember to update anything.
Net score. Your gross score minus the handicap you played off that day — the number on your card, which whoever runs the round copies into the form. Not your handicap today: if it changes later, the rounds you have already played should not change with it. The register is used only where a round was entered without it.
Stableford ties. A tie in a round's points is given the same league points and marked T, rather than the back-nine countback the finished 2025 and 2026 tables use - this pipeline only sees a gross score or a points total, not a full scorecard.
