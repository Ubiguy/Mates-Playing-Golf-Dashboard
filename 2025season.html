About this series

A note on the name. The series is called the Stableford, but the scorecards behind it hold gross strokes hole by hole, and the standings are worked out on net strokes — the same basis as the 2026 stroke play season. That keeps the two comparable.

A complete season. All fourteen rounds are now in, Game 01 on 12 April through Game 14 on 13 July. The last four were transcribed from the app's scorecards in September 2026: Game 02 and Game 07 were missing outright, Game 04 had been logged under a mistyped year, and the round the app calls Game 08 had been filed as Game 07.

Game numbers repeat each season. One round in the data belongs to 2024 — Game 02 played on 27 April 2024 — and carries its year so it never counts toward 2025.

One card was set aside. Jabar Mughal's Game 07 card records a front nine only, so the round was left out rather than completed by guesswork. The same applies to Shufqat Khan in Game 02, whose card holds no scores at all.
Scoring. Each round's winner takes 12 league points, the runner-up 11, and so on down to 1 for twelfth; thirteenth and below score nothing. A player's best 8 rounds count, so extra rounds can only help.
The comparison columns. Stableford points are the raw points from the card (2 for a net par, 3 for a birdie, and so on). Net is gross total minus the handicap played off that day. Both are totalled or averaged over the same best 8 rounds, so the three columns differ only in what they reward.
Qualifying. Eight of the fourteen rounds were needed to appear in the ranked table. Players who fell short are listed under the standings.
Ties within a round. Players level on points are separated by countback — highest back nine, then back six, then back three. That settled every tie in the season, so each place is awarded outright. Two players can still finish level over the season; those places are shared and marked T.
One exception, Game 12. Shazad Hussain and Mahmood Sadiq both returned 39 points and were level on the back nine and back six. Countback on the back three would favour Mahmood, but the round was settled as a Shazad win on the day and the result stands. Every other round follows the countback.
