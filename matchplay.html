Competition rules

Agreed at the start of the competition

1. Format. All matches are 9 holes — the front 9 — played as Stableford off full handicap.

2. Match points. 1 point for a win, ½ for a draw, 0 for a loss.

3. Bonus point. A player winning by more than 4 Stableford points takes an additional 1 bonus point.

4. Substitutions and fixture flexibility. If a player cannot fulfil a fixture the captain may replace them with another member of the same team, but no player may stand in more than twice across the competition. Matches may also be played ahead of their week where both players agree. Captains will accommodate players wherever they can, particularly around holidays — tell your captain as early as you can.

5. Spirit of the competition. This is a friendly team competition, to be played with good humour, sportsmanship and respect for your opponent.

6. Dinner and the individual prize. The losing team takes the winning team out for dinner. A trophy goes to the individual with the highest total points across the competition.

7. Winning team. The winning team receives trophies at the end of the competition.

8. Disputes. Any dispute over fixtures, results or rules is settled by the two captains, whose decision is final.
