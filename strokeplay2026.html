How this season was scored

Net strokes, not Stableford. Unlike the Stableford Singles, this championship is settled on net strokes — gross total minus the handicap played off that day. Lowest wins, so a lower number is a better number throughout this page.

Best 6 of 9. A player's standing is the average of their six best net rounds. Playing more than six can only help: the extra rounds simply give the average more to choose from, and the worst ones drop out.

Six rounds to qualify. Turnout varied from six players to fifteen across the nine rounds, so only six players reached the six-round minimum. Everyone else is listed under the table with the number of rounds they played.
Source. Computed from the hole-by-hole scorecards in the society tracker, the same data behind the handicap register.
Ties. Players level on a standing share the same position and are marked T — that is deliberate, not a glitch.
The other majors. The Stableford Singles is on the season hub; Team Games has its own page. Doubles and Singles Match Play are listed on the hub.
