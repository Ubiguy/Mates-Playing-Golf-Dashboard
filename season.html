Where the numbers come from. Stroke play and Stableford figures are computed from hole-by-hole scorecards in the society tracker. Net = gross total minus the handicap played off that day.
The Stableford Singles. The league table for these 13 rounds was read off leaderboard screenshots, and Game 02 and Game 05 were captured only down to 7th place. Full hole-by-hole scorecards have since been logged for Games 01 to 05, so those five now feed the handicap calculation; the remaining eight still sit outside it.
Earlier seasons. The 2025 Stableford series — fourteen rounds, won by Shaan Ahmed — has its own page. Handicaps are on the register. Three systems run in parallel: NHS, the society's own internal handicap and the one everyone plays off; WHS, the official England Golf index; and a calculated figure worked out from the rounds actually submitted. Only NHS is played off.
