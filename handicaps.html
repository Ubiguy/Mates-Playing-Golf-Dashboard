What each column means

Three different systems, tracked in parallel. They answer different questions and are not interchangeable. Only the first one is played off.

NHS — the society's own handicap, and the playing figure. This is the society's internal mechanism: it is largely relative, sized so members compete fairly against each other rather than against a national standard. Whenever a handicap is quoted anywhere on this site — a match play roster, an away-day card, a profile — it is the NHS figure, unless the competition in question says otherwise. Critically, the handicap recorded against every round in the tracker is the NHS handicap the player actually played off that day.

WHS — the official England Golf handicap index. Supplied per player from their England Golf record, held here for comparison. It is a national figure computed to the WHS method from cards submitted at their own clubs, so it answers a different question from NHS and will usually disagree with it. Never used as a playing figure here.

Calculated HC — what the submitted rounds actually say. Worked out here from the hole-by-hole scorecards the society has logged, as an independent third opinion on the other two. Score differential = round total minus course rating, then the average of the best N differentials with the WHS low-round adjustment applied. It stays Provisional until a player has 20 rounds on record, at which point it becomes Established. Below four rounds it swings wildly on a single card, so it is greyed out and marked thin — readable, but not something to lean on.

Historic scorecards are untouched. Net scores on the season pages were worked out from the handicap each player actually played off on the day, which is what the card recorded. Changing the register does not rewrite them.
Editing the register. Every figure lives in data.js, in the REGISTER list, one line per player. Change a number, commit, and this page and its counts update themselves.
Sorting. Players without a published figure in the column being sorted always sit at the bottom, whichever direction you sort in.
Source. All three figures come from the Player HC tab of the society tracker — NHS from the “NHS App HCP” column, WHS from the “WHS” column, and the calculated index from the logged scorecards. The NHS column is the one that counts.
Tees. Every round logged in 2025 and 2026 was played off the yellow tees, so the calculated figures for that period rest on a single consistent set of tees rather than a mixture.
