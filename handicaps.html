What each column means

NHS — supplied figure. No underlying round data held, so nothing here is computed; the number is recorded as given.

WHS — supplied figure, same as above.

Calculated HC — worked out here from the hole-by-hole scorecards the society has logged. Score differential = round total minus course rating, then the average of the best N differentials with the WHS low-round adjustment applied. It stays Provisional until a player has 20 rounds on record, at which point it becomes Established.

Too few rounds to calculate. Below four rounds the calculation swings wildly on a single card, so the player's NHS handicap stands in as the default and the cell is marked NHS. The calculated figure is still held in the data, and takes over automatically the moment a fourth card is logged.
Editing the register. Every figure lives in data.js, in the REGISTER list, one line per player. Change a number, commit, and this page and its counts update themselves.
Sorting. Players without a published figure in the column being sorted always sit at the bottom, whichever direction you sort in.
Source. All three figures come from the Player HC tab of Golf_Scores_Tracker_19.xlsx — NHS from the “NHS App HCP” column, WHS from the “WHS” column, and the calculated index from the logged scorecards.
