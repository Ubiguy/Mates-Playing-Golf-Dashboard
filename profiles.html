Reading these numbers

NHS is still the handicap. The big number on the left of each card is the player's NHS handicap, which is what they play off in society competitions. The England Golf index beside it is a separate figure, calculated by England Golf from cards submitted at their own clubs. The two rarely agree — across these nine the England Golf index sits about two shots higher on average — and nothing on this page changes what anyone plays off.

Score differential is a round's adjusted gross minus the course rating, so it measures a round against the course rather than against par. Lower is better, and the averages here run over a player's whole England Golf history, not just this season.

Peak and low. Each card shows either the highest index a player has carried or the lowest, whichever is the more telling for their history, with the date it was reached. Net change runs from the start of their record to today: a minus sign is an improvement.

Sample sizes vary a lot. Tab Rafique's index rests on six rounds, Waseem's on forty. Read a short history with the same caution the handicap register applies to its own thin figures.
